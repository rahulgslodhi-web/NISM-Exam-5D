Module 8 Mathematical Core Formulas

KaTeX Formulated
Real Rate of Return ($r_{\text{real}}$) Super Critical
$$r_{\text{real}} = \frac{1 + r}{1 + e} - 1$$

Where $r$ = nominal investment return rate and $e$ = annual inflation rate. Do NOT use simple subtraction ($r - e$) for multi-year corpus compounding!

Expenses at Retirement ($FV_{\text{exp}}$) Compounding
$$FV = PV \times (1 + e)^n$$

Where $PV$ = current annual expenses today, $e$ = pre-retirement inflation rate, and $n$ = number of years until retirement date.

Retirement Corpus ($PV_{\text{due}}$) Annuity Due
$$C = \text{Exp} \times \left[\frac{1 - (1 + r_{\text{real}})^{-N}}{r_{\text{real}}}\right] \times (1 + r_{\text{real}})$$

Annuity Due formula because retirement living expenses must be withdrawn at the beginning of every year (BGN mode on financial calculators).

SEBI Investment Adviser Code of Conduct for Retirement Planning

Under SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013, an adviser holds a strict fiduciary duty towards the retiree. Key compliance benchmarks include:

1
Fiduciary Duty

Advisers must always prioritize the client’s capital longevity over distribution incentives or commission-loaded products.

2
Risk Suitability

Retirees have reduced human capital and lower risk capacity. Portfolios must emphasize asset-liability matching (ALM).

3
SRR Mitigation

Implement bucket strategies (1-3 yrs cash buffer) to eliminate the risk of selling equity during market drawdowns early in retirement.

4
Annual Review

Mandatory annual portfolio rebalancing, healthcare escalation reviews, and nominee/estate document validation.
