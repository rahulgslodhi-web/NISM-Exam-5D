High-Frequency Numerical Formulas

Essential Formula Cards for Series X-B

Questions on HLV, Paid-Up Values, Under-Insurance, and Deductibles appear regularly in Level 2 exams.

Life Planning HLV Model

Human Life Value (Income Surplus)

HLV = PV(r, n, -(Income - Personal Expenses))

Discount the breadwinner's net economic surplus to family at the real or nominal post-tax discount rate until expected retirement age.

Policy Servicing Traditional

Paid-Up Sum Assured

Paid-Up SA = (Paid Prems / Total Prems) × Original SA

Reduces death/maturity benefit pro-rata to premiums paid. Accrued bonuses prior to paid-up date remain payable at death/maturity.

Property / Fire Contribution

Rateable Contribution Principle

Claim A = Loss × (Sum Insured A / Total Sum Insured)

When double insurance exists across multiple non-life insurers, each pays ratably based on its proportion of total insured value.

Non-Life Loss Average Clause

Principle of Average (Under-Insurance)

Payable = Loss × (Sum Insured / Actual Asset Value)

Penalizes insured for deliberately insuring less than true replacement/market value. Insured bears pro-rata fraction of the partial loss.

10 Golden Rules for Module 7 Exam Success

Exam traps and critical legal facts frequently tested in Level 2 MCQs and case studies

1. Insurable Interest Timing Trap ⭐ Must exist at inception only in Life Insurance, at time of loss only in Marine Cargo, and both inception & time of loss in Fire/General property insurance.
2. Section 45 3-Year Incontestability ⭐ After 3 years from policy inception/revival, no life insurance policy can be questioned by an insurer for ANY reason, including fraud or non-disclosure.
3. MWP Act Irrevocable Trust ⭐ Policy taken under Section 6 MWP Act creates a separate statutory trust for wife/children. Creditors, tax attachments, or insolvency cannot attach the proceeds.
4. ULIP ₹2.5 Lakh Tax Threshold ⭐ For ULIP policies issued on or after 1 February 2021, if annual aggregate premium exceeds ₹2,50,000, maturity proceeds lose Section 10(10D) tax exemption and are taxed as capital gains.
5. Free-Look Cancellation Period 15 days for physical policies, extended to 30 days for policies purchased electronically or via distance marketing. Premium refunded less stamp duty and medical expenses.
6. Super Top-Up vs Top-Up Difference ⭐ Top-up applies deductible to each individual hospitalization claim; Super Top-up aggregates all hospitalization claims across the entire policy year against one single deductible.
7. Motor Third-Party is Statutorily Unlimited Third-party bodily injury/death coverage is legally compulsory with unlimited liability, whereas third-party property damage (TPPD) is capped at ₹7,50,000 under Motor Vehicles Act.
8. Compulsory Personal Accident (CPA) Cover Mandatory CPA cover for registered owner-driver is ₹15 Lakhs for accidental death/permanent total disability.
9. PED Waiting Period & Moratorium Clause Under latest IRDAI master circular, Pre-Existing Disease (PED) waiting period is capped at 36 months (3 years), and Moratorium Period is reduced to 5 years (60 months continuous coverage).
10. Insurance Ombudsman Jurisdiction Ombudsman can entertain complaints up to ₹50 Lakhs (amended from ₹30 Lakhs). Award is binding on the insurer within 30 days, but NOT binding on the complainant.
