Section 10.1

Bank Fixed Deposits, Recurring Deposits & TDS (Section 194A)

Interest from Bank Fixed Deposits (FDs) and Recurring Deposits (RDs) is taxable under Income from Other Sources on an accrual basis at the investor's applicable slab rates.

TDS Thresholds under Section 194A

  • General Depositors (<60 yrs): TDS @ 10% if aggregate interest paid by a banking company/co-operative bank exceeds ₹40,000 in a financial year.
  • Senior Citizens (≥60 yrs): Enhanced TDS threshold of ₹50,000.
  • Non-Banking Entities (NBFCs / Corporates): Threshold is ₹5,000.
  • Absence of PAN: TDS is deducted at 20% under Section 206AA.

Form 15G and Form 15H

Form 15G: For resident individuals (<60 yrs) whose total estimated income is below the basic exemption limit and tax liability is Nil.
Form 15H: For resident senior citizens (≥60 yrs) if estimated net tax liability is Nil (even if gross interest exceeds basic exemption limit, as long as final tax is zero).

Section 10.2

Taxation of Corporate Bonds & Debentures (TDS Sec 193)

Coupon (interest) on corporate bonds is charged to tax under IFOS at applicable marginal income tax slab rates. Capital gains arise upon transfer or redemption in the secondary market.

⭐ Crucial Legal Amendment: Removal of TDS Exemption under Section 193

Prior to 1st April 2023, interest on listed debentures held in demat form was exempt from TDS. Finance Act 2023 omitted this exemption! With effect from 1st April 2023, all companies paying interest on listed debentures to resident unitholders MUST deduct TDS @ 10% under Section 193 (threshold of ₹5,000 for listed debentures paid to resident individuals by cheque/ECS).

Section 10.3 • Core RIA Syllabus
Landmark Regime

Section 50AA: Taxation of "Specified Mutual Funds"

The Finance Act 2023 introduced Section 50AA to eliminate the tax arbitrage between bank fixed deposits and pure debt mutual funds. It completely removed the indexation benefit and long-term capital gains status for specified mutual funds.

Definition of "Specified Mutual Fund" (Section 50AA):

A mutual fund scheme where not more than 35% of its total proceeds is invested in the equity shares of domestic companies. This captures all pure debt funds:

Overnight & Liquid Funds
Ultra Short / Low Duration
Corporate Bond & Banking PSU
Gilt Funds & Target Maturity

Grandfathering & Date-of-Acquisition Rules:

Units Acquired BEFORE 1st April 2023:

• Holding > 36 Months: Grandfathered! Qualifies as Long-Term Capital Asset.
• Tax Rate: 20% with indexation benefit (using Cost Inflation Index).
• Holding ≤ 36 Months: Short-Term Capital Gain at applicable slab rates.

Units Acquired ON or AFTER 1st April 2023:

• Deemed Short-Term: Gains from transfer, redemption, or maturity are statutorily deemed to be Short-Term Capital Gains under Section 50AA.
• Holding Period Irrelevant: Even if held for 5 or 10 years!
• Tax Rate: Charged at applicable marginal income tax slab rates.
• Zero Indexation: No indexation benefit allowed.

Section 10.4 • High Yield Focus
Deemed STCG

Market Linked Debentures (MLDs) under Section 50AA

Market Linked Debentures are debt securities whose returns are linked to an underlying market index (e.g. Nifty 50 or 10-year G-sec yields). Historically, MLDs held for more than 12 months were taxed @ 10% without indexation under Section 112.

Statutory Treatment of MLDs (Post 1 April 2023):
  • Section 50AA explicitly defines a Market Linked Debenture as a security having an underlying principal in the form of a debt security and where returns are linked to market variations.
  • All capital gains arising from the transfer, redemption, or maturity of an MLD on or after 1st April 2023 are deemed to be capital gains from a Short-Term Capital Asset.
  • Taxable at the investor's applicable marginal income tax slab rates, regardless of the holding duration or listing status.
Section 10.5 & 10.6

Non-Specified Hybrid Debt Funds & Special Bond Structures

Hybrid Funds (Equity >35% to <65%)

Conservative Hybrid Funds and Multi-Asset Allocation Funds having between 35% and 65% domestic equity do NOT fall under Section 50AA!
• Holding Period: > 24 Months for LTCG (Finance Act 2024).
• STCG (≤24m): Taxed at marginal slab rates.
• LTCG (>24m): Taxed at 12.5% without indexation.

Tax-Free Bonds (Section 10(15)(iv)(h))

Bonds issued by PFC, REC, NHAI, HUDCO, IRFC.
• Interest: 100% EXEMPT from income tax; no TDS.
• Capital Gains on Sale: If sold in the secondary market, capital gains are taxable (holding period 12m for listed; LTCG @ 12.5% without indexation).
