Section 9.1 • Core Regulatory Timelines
NFO Framework

New Fund Offer (NFO) Mechanics, Allotment & Cash Limits

⚡ Fast-Track Refresher: Scheme Launch Basics

A New Fund Offer (NFO) is the initial launch of a mutual fund scheme by an AMC to raise initial capital from the public. Units are offered at face value (typically ₹10 per unit, except for certain specialized or index schemes). The offer is made through the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM).

Process / Milestone Statutory Regulatory Limit Consequence / Regulatory Penalty
Open-ended Scheme NFO Maximum 15 Days Must close within 15 calendar days from opening.
ELSS (Tax Saving) NFO Maximum 30 Days Extended duration permitted specifically to facilitate Section 80C tax planning.
Close-ended Scheme NFO Maximum 15 Days Except Infrastructure Debt Funds (IDF) or special schemes approved by SEBI.
Allotment & Refund TAT Within 5 Business Days Penalty: AMC must pay interest at 15% per annum to investors for every day of delay beyond 5 business days!
Re-opening for Continuous Sale Within 5 Business Days Open-ended schemes must reopen for ongoing purchases and redemptions within 5 business days of allotment.

Modern Payment Mechanisms

  • ASBA (Application Supported by Blocked Amount): Mandatory facility for NFO applications through banks where funds stay blocked in investor's bank account until unit allotment.
  • UPI, Net Banking & NEFT/RTGS: Instant electronic funds transfer.
  • OTM (One Time Mandate) / NACH: Pre-authorized mandate for automated recurring SIP deductions without manual intervention.
  • Third-Party Payment Ban: Investments must originate from the unitholder's own bank account. Exceptions allowed only for minors (by parents/guardians up to ₹50k), employer payroll deductions, or custodian for FPIs.

Cash Transactions in Mutual Funds

To promote financial inclusion and facilitate small rural investors without immediate banking access:

Maximum ₹50,000 per Investor, per Mutual Fund, per Financial Year

⭐ Critical Exam Rule: While cash investments are accepted at designated bank branches with challans, redemptions, dividend (IDCW) payouts, and refunds are NEVER made in cash. All payouts are remitted strictly via electronic bank transfer to the verified bank account.

Section 9.2 • Super High Exam Yield ⭐
Cut-off & Realization

Applicable NAV Cut-Off Timings & The Uniform Realization Rule

Cut-off timings prevent arbitrage, unfair market-timing, and ensure all investors are treated equitably. Under SEBI regulations, purchase NAV across ALL schemes is determined strictly by the date and time when funds are credited and available for utilization by the scheme (Uniform Realization Rule), regardless of the investment amount (ticket size).

Scheme Category Transaction Type Cut-off Time Applicable NAV Allotment Principle
Liquid & Overnight Schemes Subscription / Purchase 1:30 PM NAV of the Previous Business Day
Provided application is received and funds are credited into the scheme account before 1:30 PM.
Redemption / Repurchase 3:00 PM NAV of the Same Business Day (if received ≤ 3:00 PM).
NAV of the next business day if received after 3:00 PM.
All Other Schemes
(Equity, Debt, Hybrid, FoF, Index, Solution) 		Subscription / Purchase 3:00 PM Closing NAV of the business day on which funds are available for utilization before 3:00 PM.
Applicable to ALL ticket sizes (₹500 or ₹50 Crores alike). If funds realize after 3:00 PM, next day's NAV applies.
Redemption / Repurchase 3:00 PM NAV of the Same Business Day (if received ≤ 3:00 PM).
NAV of the next business day if received after 3:00 PM. (Realization rule does NOT apply to redemptions).
Scenario A: Online Purchase with UPI

Investor submits an Equity scheme application at 2:15 PM on Monday via UPI. Funds credit instantly to scheme bank account at 2:18 PM.

Applicable NAV: Monday's Closing NAV (Both received & funds realized before 3:00 PM).

Scenario B: Cheque / Delayed NEFT

Investor submits an Equity scheme form on Monday at 11:00 AM with a cheque. The cheque clears and funds credit to scheme account on Wednesday at 11:30 AM.

Applicable NAV: Wednesday's Closing NAV (Realization date controls NAV, not form submission date!).

Scenario C: Switch-Out to Switch-In

Investor switches from Debt Fund to Equity Fund on Monday at 1:00 PM. Switch-in NAV depends on when redemption proceeds from Debt Fund reach Equity Fund.

Applicable NAV: Day on which switch-out funds reach the switch-in scheme account before 3:00 PM.

⭐ Crucial Exam Trap: Liquid Fund Friday / Weekend NAV Rule

Liquid and Overnight schemes declare NAV on all 365 days of the year (including Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays).

Friday Purchase Case: If an investor submits an application for a Liquid Fund on Friday at 12:45 PM and funds are credited before 1:30 PM:
  • Applicable NAV = Thursday's Closing NAV (Previous Business Day).
  • The investor earns returns for Friday, Saturday, and Sunday!

Conversely, if a purchase is made on a Sunday before 1:30 PM with funds credited, the applicable NAV is Saturday's NAV.

Section 9.3 • Super High Exam Yield ⭐
Account Mechanics

KYC Architecture, Minor Accounts & Minor Turning Major (MAM)

Account in the Name of a Minor

  • Sole Unitholder Mandate: The minor is strictly the sole unitholder. Joint holding is strictly prohibited under any circumstances.
  • Representation: Folio can only be operated by a Natural Guardian (Father or Mother) or a Court-Appointed Legal Guardian.
  • Proof of DOB: Mandatory submission of Birth Certificate, Passport, or school leaving certificate establishing exact date of birth.
  • Bank Account Rule: Payment must originate from and redemptions credit to a bank account in the name of the minor or a joint account of the minor with the guardian.
  • Nomination: Nomination cannot be made by or on behalf of a minor folio unitholder.

Minor Attaining Majority (MAM Protocol)

On the exact calendar date when the minor turns 18 years of age:

⚠️ Automatic Transaction Freeze:

All standing instructions (SIPs, STPs, SWPs) and debit permissions are instantly frozen. The guardian's operating authority ceases entirely by law.

Requirements to Reactivate Folio (MAM Documentation):

  • MAM Request Form signed by the unitholder (who is now an adult).
  • New PAN & Full independent KYC registration.
  • New bank account in the sole name of the major unitholder.
  • Signature attestation by bank manager or banker's confirmation letter.
KYC (Know Your Customer) Framework

PAN is the single universal identification key for mutual fund transactions. The Central KYC Registry (cKYCR) managed by CERSAI issues a 14-digit KYC Identification Number (KIN). Investors with valid KIN do not need to repeat KYC with different AMCs. For Aadhaar OTP-based e-KYC without biometric validation, the investment limit is capped at ₹50,000 per mutual fund per financial year.

Section 9.4 • Super High Exam Yield ⭐
Systematic Plans & IAF

SIP Mechanics (Rupee Cost Averaging), STP, SWP & Instant Access Facility

Systematic facilities instill financial discipline, overcome emotional bias, and optimize asset allocation over time. Understanding the mathematical mechanics of Rupee Cost Averaging is a staple exam topic.

Rupee Cost Averaging (RCA) Mathematical Proof Step-by-Step Math

Consider an investor investing ₹1,000 per month across 4 volatile market months:

Month Monthly Investment (₹) Applicable NAV (₹) Units Allotted (Amount / NAV)
Month 1 ₹1,000 ₹20.00 50.000 units
Month 2 (Dip) ₹1,000 ₹10.00 100.000 units (More units!)
Month 3 (Recovery) ₹1,000 ₹16.00 62.500 units
Month 4 (Rally) ₹1,000 ₹25.00 40.000 units (Fewer units)
Total / Result ₹4,000 Total Invested Arithmetic Mean NAV = ₹17.75 252.500 Total Units Acquired
1. Simple Arithmetic Average Price:
(₹20 + ₹10 + ₹16 + ₹25) / 4 = ₹17.75 per unit
2. Actual Average Cost per Unit (RCA):
₹4,000 / 252.50 units = ₹15.84 per unit ⭐

Key Takeaway: Average purchase cost (₹15.84) is significantly lower than average market NAV (₹17.75). RCA automatically forces investors to acquire more units during market declines and fewer during market surges.

Systematic Transfer Plan (STP)

Transfers a specified amount periodically from a source scheme (typically Liquid/Ultra Short) to a target scheme (typically Equity).

Tax Note: Each transfer is treated as a redemption from source scheme (triggering capital gains tax) and purchase in target scheme.

Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP)

Enables regular cash inflows at chosen intervals (monthly/quarterly). Unitholder specifies fixed rupee amount or fixed units to redeem.

Tax Efficiency: Only the capital appreciation component of each withdrawal is taxable, unlike IDCW which is taxed at slab rates on 100% of payout!

Instant Access Facility (IAF)

SEBI framework allowing instant online redemption in Liquid Funds via IMPS/UPI into unitholder's bank account 24x7x365.

Lower of ₹50,000 OR 90% of Folio Value
(Per Day, Per Investor)

Risk & Security

Nomination Framework, Transmission & Bank Change Cooling-Off

Nomination & Transmission Rules

  • Up to 3 Nominees: Individual folios (sole or joint) can register up to 3 nominees with exact allocation percentages summing to exactly 100%.
  • Minors as Nominees: Allowed, provided name and address of guardian are specified.
  • Non-Individuals Barred: Corporates, trusts, partnership firms, and societies cannot nominate.
  • Mandatory Opt-In or Opt-Out: Unitholders must either nominate OR submit an explicit signed declaration opting out of nomination.
  • Legal Nature: A nominee is a custodian/trustee holding units until succession is determined by court/will.
  • Transmission on Death: In joint folios, surviving unitholders become sole holders; in sole folios with nomination, units transmit to nominee upon submitting death certificate and KYC.

Change of Bank Mandate (COB) Cooling-Off

To safeguard investors from cyber-fraud, phishing, and fraudulent interception of redemption payouts:

Mandatory 10 Calendar Days Cooling-Off Period

When an investor registers a change of bank mandate:

  • Requires proof of both existing (old) and proposed (new) bank accounts.
  • If a redemption request is received within 10 calendar days of registering the new bank account, the redemption is either held until cooling-off expires OR proceeds are remitted to the old existing bank account.
Section 9.6 • Investor Reporting
Account Statements

Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) Dispatches

A Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) aggregates all financial transactions across all mutual funds and depository accounts (Demat with NSDL and CDSL) linked under a common Permanent Account Number (PAN).

Monthly CAS Dispatch

Dispatched on or before the 15th of the succeeding month to investors whose folios/demat accounts experienced active financial transactions (purchases, redemptions, switches, SIP/STP/SWP, IDCW) during the preceding month.

Half-Yearly CAS Dispatch

Dispatched on or before the 21st of the succeeding month (i.e., by October 21 for April–September, and April 21 for October–March) to investors whose folios had no financial transactions during the half-year period, reflecting unit balances and portfolio valuation.

Section 9.7 • Redressal Architecture
Investor Protection

Grievance Redressal Architecture: SCORES 2.0, SMART ODR & MITRA

SEBI and AMFI enforce a robust multi-tiered dispute resolution framework to protect retail investors, resolve service deficiencies promptly, and maintain investor confidence in mutual funds.

SCORES 2.0 Redressal System

SEBI Complaints Redress System upgraded to SCORES 2.0. Key parameters:

  • Turn-Around Time (TAT): Reduced to 21 calendar days for the entity (AMC/RTA) to respond.
  • Two-Tier Review: Tier 1 review by Designated Body (AMFI within 10 days); Tier 2 review by SEBI if complainant is not satisfied.
  • Automated complaint routing and real-time tracking.
SMART ODR Portal

Securities Market Approach for Resolution Through Online Dispute Resolution. Provides online mediation, conciliation, and independent arbitration for unsettled grievances between unitholders and market intermediaries.

AMFI MITRA Platform

Mutual Fund Investor Troubleshooter and Resolution Assistant. AMFI's centralized single-window digital portal bridging all RTAs (CAMS & KFintech) and AMCs for unified query tracking, service status, and automated inquiry routing.
