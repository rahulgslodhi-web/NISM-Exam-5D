Case studies routinely test: (1) SGB redemption with RBI (100% tax exempt) vs secondary exchange transfer (taxable), (2) Category I/II AIF pass-through vs Category III fund-level taxation at MMR, and (3) 10% Section 50C safe harbour on property sales.

Section 12.1

Real Estate Taxation, Section 50C Safe Harbour & TDS (Sec 194-IA)

Immovable property (land, residential building, commercial office) qualifies as a Long-Term Capital Asset if held for more than 24 months.

Section 50C: Stamp Duty Safe Harbour (10%)

If the actual sale consideration declared is lower than the Stamp Duty Value (SDV) assessed by the state registrar, the SDV is taken as the deemed sale price for capital gains computation.
10% Safe Harbour Rule: If the SDV does not exceed 110% of the actual consideration (a variance of up to 10%), the actual consideration is accepted without any adjustment!

Section 194-IA: Buyer's TDS Mandate

Any person (buyer) purchasing immovable property (other than agricultural land) from a resident transferor MUST deduct TDS @ 1% if the sale consideration (or SDV) is ₹50 Lakhs or more.
Deducted using Form 26QB (buyer PAN-based Challan; TAN is not required).

Section 12.2

Taxation of Physical Gold, Silver & Digital Gold

Physical gold jewellery, gold bars, silver, and digital gold are capital assets under Section 2(14).

Short-Term (≤ 24 Months) Gains are added to total income and taxed at the investor's applicable marginal income tax slab rates.
Long-Term (> 24 Months) Finance (No. 2) Act 2024 reduced the holding period from 36m to 24 months. Taxed @ 12.5% without indexation under Section 112.
Section 12.3 • High Yield Focus
Triple Tax Stream

Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs): The 3-Dimensional Tax Regime

Sovereign Gold Bonds issued by the Reserve Bank of India on behalf of the Government of India feature a unique statutory tax structure that advisors must understand thoroughly:

1. Redemption with RBI

Under Section 47(vii), any capital gain arising to an individual on redemption of SGBs (either at the 8-year maturity or through RBI's early redemption window after the 5th year) is 100% EXEMPT FROM CAPITAL GAINS TAX!

2. Secondary Market Sale

If an individual sells SGBs on the stock exchange prior to maturity: Capital gains tax DOES apply!
• Held ≤ 12 months: STCG at slab rates.
• Held > 12 months: LTCG taxed @ 12.5% without indexation.

3. Semi-Annual Interest

The guaranteed interest of 2.50% per annum paid semi-annually on the initial nominal investment is fully taxable under Income from Other Sources at the investor's slab rates.
Note: No TDS is deducted by RBI on SGB interest.

Section 115UA

Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) & InvITs

Business trusts operate under a hybrid pass-through framework under Section 115UA. Periodic distributions consist of distinct streams, each taxed differently:

Distribution Stream Tax Treatment in Unitholder's Hands TDS Provisions
1. Interest Component Taxable at marginal slab rates (Pass-through) TDS @ 10% under Section 194LBA
2. Dividend Component • Taxable at slab rates if underlying SPV opted for concessional tax under Sec 115BAA (22%).
• Exempt if SPV did NOT opt for Sec 115BAA. 		TDS @ 10% if SPV opted for 115BAA
3. Repayment of Debt (Capital Reduction) Taxable under Section 56(2)(xii) as 'Specified Sum' to the extent cumulative distributions exceed the original issue price of the units. TDS applicable on specified sum
Trading REIT/InvIT Units on Stock Exchange: Listed units held for ≤ 12 months attract STCG @ 20% under Section 111A. If held for > 12 months, LTCG is taxed @ 12.5% in excess of ₹1,25,000 under Section 112A.
Section 12.5 • Core RIA Syllabus
Sec 115UB vs 10(23FBA)

Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs): Category I, II vs Category III

The Income Tax Act draws a sharp structural distinction between Category I & II AIFs (which enjoy statutory tax pass-through status) and Category III AIFs (which are taxed at the fund entity level):

Section 115UB Pass-Through Status

Category I & Category II AIFs

Covers Venture Capital, SME Funds, Social Impact Funds, Infrastructure Funds, and Private Equity / Debt Funds.

  • • Pass-Through: Income (other than business income) is taxed directly in the hands of the investor in the same nature and proportion as if earned directly.
  • • TDS: Fund deducts TDS @ 10% under Section 194LBB on distributions to resident investors.
  • • Business Income Exception: Any business profit earned by the fund is taxed at the fund level at the Maximum Marginal Rate (MMR).
Section 10(23FBA) Fund-Level Taxation

Category III AIFs (Hedge Funds)

Covers long-short funds, PIPE strategy funds, and complex derivative trading funds.

  • • No Pass-Through: Category III AIFs are opaque vehicles. The fund itself is assessed as an AOP or Trust.
  • • Taxed at Fund Level: Income is taxed at the Maximum Marginal Rate (MMR) at the fund level before distribution.
  • • Investor Exemption: Distributions received by investors from the fund are 100% EXEMPT from tax under Section 10(23FBA).
