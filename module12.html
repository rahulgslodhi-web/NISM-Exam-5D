Advisory Practice Standard

The 6-Step Financial Planning Process (SEBI & FPSB Framework)

SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 mandates that all advice provided to clients must follow a formalized, objective, and documented suitability process. Every case study in the exam maps directly to these 6 steps:

1

Establishing Client-Adviser Relationship

Defining scope of services, fee arrangements (fixed / AUA cap of 2.5%), regulatory disclosures, fiduciary duties, and obtaining written consent.

2

Gathering Client Data & Goal Identification

Collecting financial balance sheets, tax returns, liabilities, dependent details, and quantifying financial goals with specific time horizons and current costs.

3

Analyzing Financial Status & Risk Profiling

Evaluating emergency cash reserves (3–6 months), debt-service ratios, net worth, calculating Risk Capacity vs. Tolerance, and identifying insurance shortfalls.

4

Developing Financial Plan & SAA Strategy

Formulating Strategic Asset Allocation (Core & Satellite), mathematical modeling for retirement/education corpus, tax optimization, and estate trust structures.

5

Implementing Recommendations

Adviser assists with execution through direct plans (no distribution commission permitted for RIAs), pure term life policies, and legal trust deed drafting.

6

Monitoring & Periodic Rebalancing

Reviewing portfolio performance at least annually, assessing life milestone changes, and executing corridor-based asset rebalancing (e.g. ±5% drift).

Master Formulas & Mathematical Frameworks ⭐

Tested in 70% of Numerical MCQs
Total Risk Adjusted Ch 19

Sharpe Ratio

Sharpe = (Rp - Rf) / σp

Measures excess return per unit of Total Risk (σ). Crucial for evaluating entire standalone portfolios.

Systematic Risk Adjusted Ch 19

Treynor Ratio

Treynor = (Rp - Rf) / βp

Measures excess return per unit of Systematic Risk (β). Valid when fund is part of a well-diversified multi-asset holding.

Downside Volatility Ch 19

Sortino Ratio

Sortino = (Rp - MAR) / σd

Penalizes only harmful downside volatility (σd) below the Minimum Acceptable Return (MAR).

Excess CAPM Return Ch 19

Jensen's Alpha (α)

α = Rp - [Rf + βp(Rm - Rf)]

Measures manager stock-picking skill. Positive α indicates the fund generated returns beyond its systematic risk level.

Inflation Adjustment Ch 20

Real Rate of Return (r)

r = [(1 + i) / (1 + g)] - 1

Fisher formula for retirement planning: i = nominal investment return, g = inflation rate. Essential for annuity corpus calculations.

Life Insurance Adequacy Ch 20

HLV (Income Replacement)

HLV = PV(Annual Earnings - Personal Expenses, n, r)

Discounting the breadwinner's net financial contribution to family across remaining working years at real rate of return.
