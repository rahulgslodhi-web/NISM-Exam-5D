Section 6.1 • Fast-Track Refresher
Channels & Infrastructure

Mutual Fund Distribution Channels & Industry Platforms

Mutual fund distribution serves as the crucial financial bridge between Asset Management Companies (AMCs) and diverse retail, high-net-worth, and institutional investors. Distributors are market intermediaries who facilitate investor onboarding, scheme selection, compliance execution, and ongoing portfolio servicing.

1. Individual MFDs (Independent Financial Advisors - IFAs)

Sole proprietors and self-employed professionals. They represent the bedrock of retail mutual fund penetration in Tier-2, Tier-3, and B-30 cities, providing doorstep personalized financial guidance and relationship-driven distribution.

2. Commercial Banks & Small Finance Banks

Institutional distributors leveraging extensive brick-and-mortar branch networks and captive depositor bases. They cross-sell third-party mutual fund schemes to retail and corporate clients through dedicated relationship managers holding EUINs.

3. National Distributors (NDs) & NBFCs

Large corporate distribution entities (e.g., NJ India, Prudent Corporate) operating organized pan-India branch networks and sub-broker franchisee models. NBFCs distribute mutual funds alongside wealth products, loans, and bonds.

4. Stock Brokers & Exchange Platforms

Registered stock brokers utilize exchange-operated transaction engines—BSE StAR MF, NSE NMF II, and MCX-SX—allowing investors to transact seamlessly in demat or physical (Statement of Account - SoA) form with automated settlement.

SEBI Regulatory Framework: Execution Only Platforms (EOPs)

In 2023, SEBI instituted a dedicated regulatory framework for digital aggregators operating as Execution Only Platforms (EOPs) to ensure transparency and prevent conflict of interest in digital Direct and Regular plan distribution:

Category 1 EOP (Agent of AMCs)

• Registered with AMFI.
• Acts as an agent of the Asset Management Companies.
• Revenue source: Receives flat transaction fees directly from AMCs.
• Cannot charge transaction fees to investors.

Category 2 EOP (Agent of Investors)

• Registered with SEBI as a Stock Broker.
• Acts as an agent of the client/investor.
• Revenue source: Can charge nominal transaction fees directly to investors.
• Prohibited from receiving any fees or commissions from AMCs.

Centralized Industry Infrastructure: MFU & MFCentral
MF Utilities (MFU)

• Industry utility platform promoted by AMCs under the aegis of AMFI.
• Allots a unique Common Account Number (CAN) to investors.
• Facilitates single-window KYC, single-form, and single-mandate/payment execution across schemes of multiple participating fund houses.

MFCentral

• Joint digital initiative created by RTAs (CAMS & KFintech) under SEBI's mandate.
• Offers a unified consolidated portfolio view across all AMCs linked to an investor's PAN and mobile number.
• Executes digital non-commercial service requests (NCSR)—such as change of bank mandate, address, email, nomination, and transmission—as well as commercial buy/sell transactions.

Mandatory Prerequisites to Distribute Mutual Funds

Under SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, no person or entity is permitted to market, solicit, distribute, or execute transactions in mutual fund schemes without meeting four statutory regulatory prerequisites:

Pillar 1 Certification
1. NISM Certification

Distributors must pass the NISM-Series-V-A: Mutual Fund Distributors exam (or NISM-Series-V-D: Mutual Fund - Specialized Investment Fund Distributors for specialized funds like SIFs).

⭐ Grandfathering / CPE Exemption: Individuals who have reached the age of 50 years OR have completed at least 10 years of experience in mutual fund distribution as on May 31, 2010 are exempt from the exam and can qualify via Continuing Professional Education (CPE) training.
Pillar 2 Biometric Identity
2. Know Your Distributor (KYD)

To eliminate dummy agencies and benami entities, AMFI mandates thorough KYD verification prior to issuing or renewing an ARN.

• Verification of PAN card, proof of identity, address proof, and bank credentials.

• Biometric Verification: In-person impression of the index finger of the right hand recorded at CAMS/KFintech Point of Service (PoS) centers.

Pillar 3 AMFI Registration
3. AMFI Registration Number (ARN)

The primary license issued by AMFI authorizing the distributor to solicit mutual fund investments and empanel with AMCs.

• Minimum Age: Applicant must be at least 18 years of age.

• Validity: Valid for 3 years from the date of issuance.

• 100% Online Mandate: Effective October 1, 2024, all fresh registrations and renewals of ARN/EUIN are processed exclusively online via the AMFI portal.

Pillar 4 Accountability
4. Employee Unique ID (EUIN)

Mandatory for all relationship managers, sales executives, and advisory employees of banks, corporate distributors, and NDs.

• Mandatory Form Quoting: EUIN must be cited on every application slip to establish individual accountability in case of mis-selling.

• Blank EUIN Declaration: If an investor executes without any employee advice, they must sign a mandatory declaration confirming the transaction was "execution-only".

Step-by-Step Licensing Flow
Step 1 Pass NISM Exam (or CPE if eligible)
Step 2 Complete KYD & Biometric at PoS
Step 3 Apply Online for ARN (Age ≥ 18)
Step 4 Empanel with AMCs & Start Distribution

⭐ Remuneration Mechanics

100% Full Trail Commission Model & B-30 Incentive Framework

Total Ban on Upfront Commissions (SEBI Mandate w.e.f. Oct 2018) Zero Upfront

SEBI completely prohibited upfront commissions and the upfronting of trail commissions across all mutual fund schemes. All distribution commissions must be paid strictly on a trail basis out of the scheme's statutory Total Expense Ratio (TER). AMCs and sponsors are legally barred from paying any extra commission, soft dollars, gifts, or incentives from their corporate balance sheets.

Daily Trail Commission Computation Formula
Daily Trail Commission = [ Daily Closing Net Assets (AUM) × Trail Rate p.a. ] / 365
Indexed to NAV: As the fund's NAV rises with market growth, the distributor's trail commission increases proportionately. If NAV falls, commission drops.
No Commission on Self-Business: Distributors earn zero commission on investments made in their own name, sole proprietorship, or where they are first holders.
Step-by-Step Numerical Illustration

Suppose an investor purchases units worth ₹10,00,000 through an MFD in an Equity Scheme with an agreed annual trail rate of 0.73% p.a.

Day 1 Trail = (₹10,00,000 × 0.0073) / 365 = ₹20.00 per day

After 3 years, if good market performance and compounding lift the client's AUM to ₹16,00,000:

New Daily Trail = (₹16,00,000 × 0.0073) / 365 = ₹32.00 per day (+60% trail growth without needing fresh capital!)

This dynamic aligns distributor interests directly with client wealth creation rather than transaction volume churning.

B-30 (Beyond Top 30 Cities) & Women Investor Incentives

AMFI Operational Framework

Regulatory Background: In March 2023, SEBI suspended the earlier framework allowing AMCs to charge up to 30 bps additional TER for B-30 inflows due to widespread churning and application-splitting abuses.

Current Structure (Funded from 2 bps Investor Education Fund): To continue incentivizing geographic dispersion and women investor inclusion, AMFI instituted a targeted incentive structure:

Lump Sum Investments

• 1% of the first application amount, subject to a maximum cap of ₹2,000.

• Clawback Mandate: The investment must remain invested for a minimum holding period of 1 year (365 days); early redemption triggers a 100% clawback.

SIP Investments

• 1% of the total first-year committed investment amount, subject to a maximum cap of ₹2,000.

• Only applicable to first-time individual retail PANs from B-30 locations or new women investors. Dual incentives for the same PAN are strictly prohibited.

Abolition of Transaction Charges: In August 2025, SEBI completely discontinued the earlier practice of deducting transaction charges (₹100 for existing / ₹150 for new investors) from subscription amounts, ensuring 100% of investor capital enters the scheme units.
GST Architecture on Mutual Fund Distribution (18% Rate)
Distributor Annual Turnover GST Registration Status Tax Collection Mechanism
≤ ₹20 Lakhs p.a. (or ≤ ₹10 Lakhs in Special Category States) Exempt / Unregistered Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM): The AMC directly discharges the 18% GST liability to the Government on behalf of the distributor.
> ₹20 Lakhs p.a. (or > ₹10 Lakhs in Special Category States) Mandatorily Registered Forward Charge: MFD issues a valid GST tax invoice with 18% GST added; AMC pays commission + GST, and MFD remits GST to the Government.
Section 6.4 • Regulatory Thresholds
Governance & Risk

AMC Due Diligence Thresholds for Large Distributors

To protect investor interests and mitigate systemic risks, SEBI mandates that AMCs must implement an enhanced annual due diligence process for distributors who cross any ONE of the following five statutory thresholds:

1
Geographical Spread

Operating presence in more than 20 locations / cities across India.

2
Non-Institutional AUM

Mobilized non-institutional (retail + HNI) AUM exceeding ₹100 Crore.

3
Gross Industry Commission

Gross commission received across all mutual funds exceeding ₹1 Crore p.a.

4
Single-AMC Commission

Gross commission received from a single AMC exceeding ₹50 Lakhs p.a.

5 ⭐
Abnormal Churn / Portfolio Turnover

Portfolio turnover ratio of client assets that is more than two times (2x) the industry average (indicative of churning for commissions).

Key Scope of AMC Distributor Due Diligence Review:
  • Review of business model, corporate governance, organizational chart, and financial solvency.
  • Verification of EUIN allotment and ongoing NISM/KYD compliance of sales force.
  • Assessment of customer grievance redressal mechanism, complaints ratio, and resolution timelines.
  • Adherence to AML (Anti-Money Laundering), PMLA, and FATCA/CRS verification standards.
  • Examination of product concentration, excessive scheme switching, and retail churning patterns.

⭐ Regulatory Segregation

Regulatory Segregation: MFDs vs. Registered Investment Advisers (RIAs)

Under SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 and subsequent amendments, SEBI established a watertight boundary between investment advisory and product distribution to eliminate conflicts of interest:

Parameter Mutual Fund Distributor (MFD) Registered Investment Adviser (RIA)
Registration Body AMFI (allotted an ARN) SEBI (allotted an INA registration)
Primary Role Distribution & incidental execution of mutual funds Comprehensive fiduciary financial planning & advice
Revenue Source Trail commission paid exclusively by AMCs from TER Direct fee charged to the client (fixed or % of AUA)
Permitted Scheme Plans Regular Plans Only (where commission is embedded) Direct Plans Only (zero distribution commission)
Client Advisory Fee Zero. Cannot charge any fee directly to investors Permitted up to SEBI caps (max ₹1.25L flat or 2.5% AUA)
Nomenclature & Title ⭐ Must use "AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor". Prohibited from using "Adviser", "Wealth Manager", or "Planner" Can officially use "Investment Adviser"
Individual Segregation An individual cannot hold dual licenses. An individual can ONLY be either an MFD or an RIA. Corporate entities must maintain strict arm's-length departmental and client-family level segregation.
Mandatory Tagline & Font Size Rule (SEBI & AMFI Mandate)

Mutual Fund Distributors must prominently display the exact tagline: "AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor" in a clear, legible font of at least font size 12 across all visiting cards, letterheads, signboards, websites, social media handles, email signatures, and promotional materials.

AMFI Code of Conduct for Distributors
• Principle of Suitability Recommend schemes strictly aligned with client risk tolerance, horizon, and financial goals. Avoid high-risk steering for commission.
• Prohibition of Rebating Passing back or rebating commission to investors in cash, gifts, or kind is strictly banned and causes ARN cancellation.
• Mandatory Commission Disclosure Disclose all commissions received and receivable across competitive scheme options to investors prior to transaction.
• Prompt Remittance & Truthful Ads Never hold investor funds or checks in distributor's own name; ensure marketing complies with SEBI advertising guidelines.

Nomination Rights & Distributor Change Regulations

Commission Payment to MFD Nominee

Upon the demise of an individual mutual fund distributor, the ongoing trail commission on the assets mobilized during the deceased distributor's lifetime is transferred and paid to the registered nominee or legal heir:

⭐ Crucial Exam Rule:

A nominee or legal heir DOES NOT require an ARN or NISM certification to claim and receive ongoing trail commission on assets mobilized by the deceased MFD prior to death.

*Note: If the nominee wishes to actively manage the folios, solicit fresh investments, or have the AUM transferred to their own code, they must pass NISM, complete KYD, and obtain an ARN.

Change of Distributor & 12-Month Cooling-Off

Investors have the unconstrained freedom to change their distributor or migrate to Direct plans at any point in time:

1. No NOC Required:

AMCs are legally prohibited from demanding a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the existing distributor. The investor's written request must be processed directly.

2. 12-Month Cooling-Off Period:

To eliminate unethical client poaching, no trail commission is paid to either the old or new distributor for 12 months following the transfer. The new distributor earns trail only from month 13 onwards.
