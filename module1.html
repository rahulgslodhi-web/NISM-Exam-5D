Module 1: Mutual Fund & SIF Distributors

Comprehensive study of mutual fund concepts, scheme categorization (equity, debt, hybrid, and the newly notified Specialized Investment Fund framework), SEBI/AMFI regulatory mandates, valuation of securities, NAV & TER computation, updated taxation slabs, investor lifecycle operations, and quantitative scheme selection metrics.
