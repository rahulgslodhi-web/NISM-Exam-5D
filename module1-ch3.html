Section 3.1 • ⚡ Fast-Track Refresher
Public Trust Doctrine

Structure of Mutual Funds in India (The Public Trust)

In India, mutual funds are NOT set up as companies or partnerships. Under the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996, all mutual funds must be constituted as Public Trusts governed by the Indian Trusts Act, 1882. The mutual fund trust is legally brought into existence through a Trust Deed executed by the Sponsor in favour of the Trustees, registered under the Indian Registration Act, 1908.

Settlor / Sponsor

The promoter entity who conceives the fund, executes the registered Trust Deed, and establishes the mutual fund as a public trust.

Trustees

Legal custodians holding scheme assets in fiduciary capacity solely for the benefit of investors. Accountable to SEBI.

Beneficiaries

The investors (Unitholders) who pool their capital into the schemes. They hold equitable ownership in scheme assets.

Why the Trust Structure? The Principle of "Ring-Fencing" & Bankruptcy Remoteness

Because a mutual fund is a Trust, the money pooled from investors does not belong to the Asset Management Company (AMC) nor to the Sponsor. The assets of each scheme are legally separated ("ring-fenced") from the corporate balance sheet of the AMC. If the Sponsor or AMC goes bankrupt or faces liquidation, creditors of the AMC/Sponsor have zero claim over the mutual fund scheme assets. Unitholders' wealth remains 100% protected and intact.

Section 3.2 • ⭐ Super High Exam Yield
Eligibility Routes

The Mutual Fund Sponsor: Promoter of the Fund

The Sponsor is any person or entity who, acting alone or in combination with another corporate body, establishes a mutual fund. Under SEBI regulations, the Sponsor must satisfy stringent eligibility norms.

Route A: Standard Eligibility Route Financial Track Record
  • 5-Year Track Record: Must be in the business of financial services for not less than 5 continuous years.
  • Positive Net Worth: Net worth must be positive in all immediately preceding 5 years.
  • Liquid Net Worth Rule: Sponsor's net worth in the immediately preceding year must exceed the capital contributed by it to the AMC.
  • Profitability Track Record: Net profit after tax in all 5 immediately preceding years, with an average net annual profit after tax of at least ₹10 Crore (statutory baseline: profit in at least 3 out of 5 preceding years including the 5th year).
  • Minimum Contribution: Must contribute at least 40% of the net worth of the Asset Management Company.
Route B: Alternate Eligibility Route PE / FinTech / Tech
  • For Non-Financial / Tech Promoters: Designed for Private Equity (PE) funds, fintechs, and tech entities lacking 5-year financial services track record or historical profits.
  • Net Worth Threshold: Sponsor must possess a minimum net worth of ₹150 Crore.
  • 5-Year Shareholding Lock-in: Minimum 40% equity shareholding in AMC must be locked in for 5 years from registration.
  • Higher AMC Net Worth Requirement: AMC must maintain a continuous net worth of ₹100 Crore (instead of ₹50 Cr) until the AMC records positive profit after tax in 5 consecutive years.
Route C: Self-Sponsored AMC Framework (New SEBI Category)

SEBI permits mature, self-sustaining AMCs to continue operations as a Self-Sponsored AMC when the original promoter/sponsor wishes to disinvest or exit. To qualify:

• Operating as an AMC for at least 5 years
• Net profit after tax in all 5 preceding years
• Average annual PAT of at least ₹100 Crore over 5 years
"Fit and Proper Person" Mandate (Schedule II)

A sponsor must be a "fit and proper person" under SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008. If the sponsor or any of its directors has been convicted of economic offences, fraud, securities market violations, or debarred by SEBI, registration will be rejected.

Section 3.3 • ⭐ Super High Exam Yield
Fiduciary Custodians

Board of Trustees & Trustee Company: Guardians of Investors

Trustees hold the mutual fund assets in trust for the benefit of unitholders. Their role is purely fiduciary. They do NOT manage the daily portfolio investments—they oversee and supervise the AMC to ensure compliance with SEBI regulations and scheme objectives.

Legal Form of Trustees

Trustees can be structured either as a Board of Trustees (individual trustees) or as a corporate body called a Trustee Company (incorporated under the Companies Act). In modern Indian mutual funds, virtually all funds operate through a Trustee Company for institutional continuity and corporate governance.

Independence Threshold (⭐ Crucial Number!)

At least two-thirds (2/3rd or 66.67%) of the members of the Board of Trustees or Directors of the Trustee Company must be independent (persons who are not associates or related to the Sponsor, AMC, or their subsidiaries).

Mandatory Trustee Eligibility & Restriction Rules:

1. Prior Approval of SEBI

No person can be appointed as a Trustee without the prior written approval of SEBI.

2. Single Fund Trustee Rule

A person appointed as a trustee of one mutual fund cannot be appointed as a trustee of any other mutual fund.

3. AMC Personnel Disqualified

No director, officer, or employee of the Asset Management Company can be appointed as a Trustee of the fund.

Key Statutory Powers & Obligations of Trustees:

  • Appoint and Supervise the AMC: Enter into an Investment Management Agreement (IMA) with the AMC, ensuring all actions comply with the Trust Deed and SEBI regulations.
  • Power to Terminate AMC: The appointment of an AMC can be terminated by a majority of the Trustees OR by 75% of unitholders of the scheme.
  • Mandatory Meeting Frequency: Trustees must meet at least once every two calendar months, and hold at least six meetings in a financial year to review scheme operations and compliance.
  • Appoint Custodian & Scheme Auditor: Trustees appoint an independent Custodian to safeguard scheme assets and appoint an independent Scheme Auditor (which must be different from AMC's auditor).
  • Prior Approval for Fundamental Attribute Changes: Any change in fundamental attributes of a scheme, roll-over of close-ended schemes, or scheme merger requires prior approval of the Trustees (and SEBI), followed by a 30-day exit window to unitholders without exit load.
  • Independent Due Diligence on NFOs: Trustees must independently review and approve every New Fund Offer (NFO) document before it is filed with SEBI.
  • Reporting to SEBI: Submit half-yearly reports to SEBI on mutual fund activities, non-performing assets (NPAs), investor complaints, and unitholder interest protection.
Section 3.4 • ⭐ Super High Exam Yield
Investment Arm

Asset Management Company (AMC): The Operating Engine

The AMC is a company registered under the Companies Act, 2013, appointed by the Trustees through an Investment Management Agreement (IMA) to manage the day-to-day investments of the schemes.

Net Worth Requirement (Continuous)

The AMC must maintain a continuous minimum net worth of not less than ₹50 Crore deployed in assets (capital + free reserves). Under the Alternate Sponsor Route, this net worth requirement rises to ₹100 Crore until the AMC generates profits for 5 consecutive years.

AMC Board Independence (⭐ Crucial Number!)

At least 50% (half) of the members of the Board of Directors of the AMC must be independent directors (not associated with the sponsor, trustees, or subsidiaries). Furthermore, the Chairman of the AMC cannot be a trustee of any mutual fund.

AMC Functional Hierarchy & Key Personnel:

1. MD & CEO

Overall administrative and strategic executive responsible for AMC operations, reporting to AMC Board.

2. Chief Investment Officer (CIO)

Heads the investment team (equities, debt, alternatives); ensures all portfolios align with scheme SIDs.

3. Fund Managers & Analysts

Research analysts generate buy/sell recommendations; fund managers make specific asset allocation decisions.

4. Dealers & Dealing Room

Execute trades at best market prices. Dealing room has restricted access, no personal mobiles, and mandatory recorded lines.

5. Compliance Officer

Ensures adherence to SEBI Act & regulations. Has direct reporting access to the Board of Trustees on compliance failures.

6. Chief Risk Officer (CRO)

Monitors Risk Management Framework (RMF) across credit, liquidity, market, operational, information security, and cyber risk.

Crucial Regulatory Safeguards on AMC & Employees:

"Skin in the Game" (Mandatory Co-investment)
  • Designated Employees: Key personnel (CIO, Fund Managers, Research Analysts, Dealers) must receive at least 20% of their net salary / perks in the form of units of the schemes they manage, locked in for 3 years or tenure of scheme.
  • AMC Capital: The AMC itself must invest mandatory seed capital in each scheme based on the risk-o-meter rating.
Restrictions on AMC Activities & Personal Trading
  • Competing Businesses: AMC can ONLY undertake asset management activities (MF, Portfolio Management - PMS, AIF, advisory services, offshore funds). It cannot engage in commercial lending or trading on own account.
  • No Cross-Trusteeship: AMC cannot act as trustee of any other mutual fund.
  • Front-Running Prohibition: Pre-clearance for personal trades; minimum 30-day holding period; blackout periods during scheme rebalancing.

Section 3.6 • ⭐ Super High Exam Yield
Arm's-Length Firewall

The Custodian & The Dual-Auditor Principle

SEBI enforces strict structural firewalls to prevent fraud, misappropriation, or self-dealing between the AMC, Custodian, and Auditors.

Role of the Custodian (Appointed by Trustees)

The Custodian is responsible for the safe custody of all scheme securities (shares, bonds, gold, government securities, money market instruments). All purchase and sale transactions are settled through the custodian on a Delivery versus Payment (DvP) basis.

1. Corporate Actions Tracking

Tracks dividends, interest payments, bonus shares, rights issues, and stock splits, ensuring scheme receives all entitlements.

2. Strict Arm's-Length Criteria ⭐

If the Sponsor or its associates hold 50% or more voting rights or control the board of a custodian, that custodian CANNOT be appointed for that mutual fund.

⭐ The Dual-Auditor Independence Rule (Crucial Exam Question!)

Under SEBI MF Regulations, the accounts of the mutual fund schemes must be kept entirely separate from the accounts of the AMC:

Scheme Auditor

• Appointed by the Trustees.
• Audits scheme balance sheets, portfolio books, and NAV computations.

AMC Auditor

• Appointed by the AMC Board / Shareholders.
• Audits the corporate balance sheet and P&L of the management company.

⚠️ Law: The Scheme Auditor CANNOT be the same auditing firm or associate firm as the AMC Auditor! Simultaneous audit of both the AMC and any scheme of the same fund by the same auditor is strictly prohibited.
Section 3.7 • Fast-Track
Investor Servicing

Registrar & Transfer Agents (RTAs) & MF Central

The Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA) is registered with SEBI under the SEBI (Registrars to an Issue and Share Transfer Agents) Regulations, 1993. The RTA manages back-office investor accounting and record-keeping on behalf of the AMC. While AMCs may maintain in-house RTA divisions, most Indian AMCs outsource this function to specialist RTAs.

1. Folio Maintenance

Maintains complete investor database (folios), demographic details, bank mandates, nominations, and tax status.

2. Transaction Processing

Processes subscriptions, redemptions, switches, SIPs, STPs, and SWPs, generating transaction confirmations.

3. Statements & Payouts

Dispatches monthly Consolidated Account Statements (CAS), dividend credits, and redemption payouts to investor accounts.

Dominant RTAs in India:

Two major institutions service over 99% of the Indian mutual fund industry:
• CAMS (Computer Age Management Services Ltd.)
• KFin Technologies Ltd. (KFintech)

MF Central (Unified Platform)

Under SEBI directive, CAMS and KFintech jointly developed MF Central—a unified digital platform enabling unitholders to view their entire MF portfolio across all AMCs and execute financial/non-financial service requests seamlessly.

Section 3.8 • Back-Office Pillars
Valuation & NAV

Fund Accounting, Approved Valuation Agencies & Depositories

1. Fund Accountant

Calculates the daily Net Asset Value (NAV) per unit of each scheme. Collects closing market prices, accrues daily interest and dividend income, factors in expenses within regulatory Total Expense Ratio (TER) limits, and computes units outstanding. Can be in-house or outsourced to custodian banks.

2. Approved Valuation Agencies (⭐ Exam Question!)

For fair and uniform valuation of illiquid, thinly traded, or non-traded debt securities, AMFI has appointed two independent valuation agencies: CRISIL Ltd. and ICRA Ltd. They publish daily valuation matrices to ensure AMCs cannot manipulate debt scheme NAVs.

3. KYC Registration Agencies (KRAs)

Entities registered under SEBI KRA Regulations (e.g., CVL, NDML, CAMS KRA, KFin KRA, DotEx). They maintain centralized digitized KYC records. Once an investor completes KYC with any SEBI intermediary, they do NOT need to repeat KYC with any other mutual fund.

4. Depositories & DPs (NSDL & CDSL)

National Securities Depository Ltd. (NSDL) and Central Depository Services Ltd. (CDSL) hold mutual fund units in dematerialized (Demat) form. Mandatory for holding Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) and optional for all regular mutual fund schemes.

Section 3.10 • ⭐ High Exam Yield
Exam Trap Alert!

Role and Statutory Status of AMFI

Formed in 1995, the Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI) is the apex trade association representing all SEBI-registered Asset Management Companies.

THE #1 MOST TESTED QUESTION ON AMFI:

AMFI is an industry association. It is NEITHER a statutory regulatory authority NOR a Self-Regulatory Organisation (SRO) under the SEBI Act.

In multiple exam questions, distractors claim "AMFI is the regulator of mutual funds" or "AMFI is an SRO for distributors". Both are completely false! SEBI is the sole statutory regulator of mutual funds in India.

Core Functions of AMFI:

1. Distributor Registration (ARN)

Allots AMFI Registration Number (ARN) to qualified distributors upon passing NISM Series V-A / V-D exams, along with EUIN for sales employees.

2. Professional Standards & ACE

Maintains the AMFI Code of Ethics (ACE) for AMCs and the Code of Conduct for Intermediaries, recommending best operational practices.

3. Nationwide Investor Education

Spearheads the iconic "Mutual Funds Sahi Hai" multi-media awareness campaign, financed by the mandatory 1-2 bps investor education levy.

4. Data & Valuation Services

Publishes monthly industry AUM, folio, and scheme inflow/outflow metrics. Appoints independent valuation agencies (CRISIL and ICRA) for debt valuation.

Section 3.11 • Specialized Focus
SIF Governance

Governance of Specialized Investment Funds (SIF)

Under the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs) are housed within the same Mutual Fund Trust and AMC structure as traditional mutual funds. However, due to their higher complexity, dynamic hedging strategies, and large minimum ticket size (₹10 Lakhs), SEBI enforces dedicated operational safeguards:

1. Dedicated Fund Managers

Fund managers handling SIF strategies must possess specialized track records in derivatives, arbitrage, or alternative assets, separate from retail mutual fund schemes.

2. Enhanced Risk Supervision

The AMC's Chief Risk Officer (CRO) must conduct daily stress tests and scenario simulations, monitoring leverage and gross exposure limits mandated under SIF guidelines.

3. Specialized Custody & DvP

The Custodian must support complex corporate actions, derivative margins, collateral pledging, and short-selling settlements under SEBI's SLB framework.
